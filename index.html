MIRI Portable Suitcase Kitchen - new project

What it is: A single-handed, portable suitcase kitchen for one person or one family. Compact, movable, clean.

  • Outer shell: copper-nickel, with copper engraved manual that will not bleach in sun or fade from touches
  • Inner food vessel: see-through borosilicate glass - you see what you are eating
  • Syphon vein inside - quiet water flow, no pump noise
  • Flat copper heating plate with electrodes - no open fire, no smoke
  • Bluetooth as a protected switch only - it sparks permission, battery and sun provide the heat
  • Small lamps: blue for cold, warm amber for warm, off for calm
  • Tiny copper-dust waterproof umbrella above
  • Small toolbox next to the battery: screwdriver, extra screws, repair parts
  • Three pockets: clean food dust pocket, warm water pocket, sealed trash pocket for used bags

Food as dust: MIRI Soups, MIRI Dust Soup, MIRI Soup Cup, Lemonade Water Mix, Miri Sun Rolls (Always On Time), soups, fruits, meat, fish, dustified pickles. Light, long keeping, rehydrated in warm water.

Full circle: Carry water - warm water - eat - clean - carry your trash - recycle the bag. Teach survival, safety, generosity. Teach where things are made and where they go after usage.
